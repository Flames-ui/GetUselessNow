# GETUSELESS 🤡

### The internet's most useless website.

https://www.getuseless.name.ng/

We have productivity apps.

We have AI tools.

We have project management software.

We have websites that promise to change your life.

So we built the opposite.

**GETUSELESS.**

A ridiculously unnecessary collection of interactive experiences designed to accomplish absolutely nothing.

## What can you do?

🖲️ Fight with a button that doesn't want to be clicked.

🥔 Interact with a potato.

🪨 Stare at a rock.

🕳️ Throw things into a completely unnecessary hole.

🐌 Watch an extremely slow snail race.

🧠 Take a useless IQ test.

🔮 Receive completely unnecessary predictions.

🚪 Walk through an infinite collection of questionable doors.

🫧 Pop virtual bubble wrap.

🌑 Stare into the void.

💡 Make completely unnecessary decisions.

And much more.

## Why does this exist?

It doesn't need to.

That's the entire point.

The internet is full of websites trying to make you more productive.

GETUSELESS exists to give you permission to waste a few minutes.
